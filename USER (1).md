# USER

## Who I am

- Call me Pey. Full name Peyton Cooper; I sign emails as Peyton.
- New Member Consultant at Athena Alliance, covering prospecting, outreach, discovery calls, and member onboarding.
- Working toward a full-time sales role.

## How I take in information

Short and scannable. Give me a quick list of who needs a ping today with a one-line reason for each, with the drafts ready right underneath.

## When I'm sharpest and what's non-negotiable

- I'm sharpest around 9:30 to 11:30 AM PT, but calls often fill that window, so have my daily pipeline review ready first thing in the morning.
- I can't work my pipeline during calls or meetings, and I don't want it squeezed into 30-minute gaps between them.
- Never fill my whole calendar. I need breaks and time to eat.

## How I decide

Data first. I decide who needs a ping by timing and activity: days since the last touch, whether she replied, and whether she RSVP'd to or attended an event. Questions from a prospect always get a reply right away.

## What people misread about me

My patience with prospects gets mistaken for not pushing to close. It's intentional. I meet her where she is, and I still guide every conversation toward a decision.

## Where I go wrong and how to push back

1. I give too many options, or default to an event invite, when she already has what she needs to decide. When a prospect looks ready, draft the close with one clear next step (the join link) and tell me plainly: "She's ready. An event invite may slow her down."
2. I take on too many things at once and lose focus on closing, which matters most. When other work is crowding out my pipeline, say so directly and remind me that closing comes first.

## Never assume

- That a prospect who went quiet isn't interested.
- That she's seen the recap email.
- That pricing or discounts are the same for everyone.
- That I've already followed up somewhere else, like a call or LinkedIn.

## Off limits

Sending any ping without my permission. If I grant access to talk to a specific person without me, go for it, but not until I say so.
